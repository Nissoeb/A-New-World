A New World

A Living Progression Fantasy RPG

************************************************

A thousand years ago, reality changed forever.

Rifts opened across the world. Monsters emerged. Ancient Dungeons appeared beneath ruined civilizations. Humanity survived by adapting to the Core System — a power structure based on cultivation, resonance, and evolving abilities.

Now civilization is built on:

dungeon exploration
shard economies
powerful factions
cultivation academies
political conflict
dangerous monster ecosystems

There are no fixed classes.

Every cultivator builds their own path through:

affinities
Sub Cores
resonance
combat experience
relationships
survival

This world is designed as a living simulation where:

factions evolve independently
monsters migrate and mutate
rumors spread
relationships change naturally
politics and economies shift over time
events continue even without the player

You are not a chosen hero.

You begin as one person among millions trying to survive, grow stronger, and find a place in a dangerous world shaped by power, ambition, and consequence.

Your choices matter.

The world reacts.

****************************************************************************************************************

Players may also access detailed in-world status systems at any time, including:

character stats
resonance condition
relationship status
reputation
inventory
active cores
progression tracking
faction standing
injuries and conditions

The experience blends immersive world simulation with optional LitRPG-style HUD elements.

****************************************************************************************************************

Recommended Experience

For the best experience:

- Use ChatGPT dark mode
- Use the built-in read aloud narration
- Take your time
- Treat the world seriously
- Focus on immersion, relationships, and long-term progression

The experience is designed to feel like:
an interactive fantasy audiobook
a living MUD
and an emergent progression fantasy world.

****************************************************************************************************************

Important Note

This world is generated dynamically through AI and may occasionally produce inconsistencies during extremely long playthroughs.

The focus of the experience is immersion, emotional continuity, world reactivity, and emergent storytelling rather than perfect mechanical simulation.
